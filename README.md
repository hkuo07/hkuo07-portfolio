# About me
Hi! I'm Huan-Tzu Kuo. I am a master's student in Information Systems Management at Carnegie Mellon University. I graduated from a university in Taiwan with a bachelor's degree in Information Management.

I'm particularly interested in data analytics and how we can turn complex information into insights that people can easily understand and use.

# What I hope to learn
Through this course, I hope to learn how to extract meaningful insights from complex datasets and present key information effectively to different audiences. I also want to improve my ability to select appropriate visualization techniques based on the characteristics of the data. Additionally, I hope to develop my data storytelling skills by learning how to present data in a clear and accessible way.

After graduation, I hope to pursue a career in data analytics, where I can apply these skills to solve real-world problems and support better decision-making.
